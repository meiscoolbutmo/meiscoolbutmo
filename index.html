What is the purpose of this website?

This is my personal website! It acts as information about me and my YouTube channel, a source of free-to-use planetary texture maps, and a window to my other socials and content online.

Who I am

I am meiscoolbutmo, an artist, small youtuber, scratcher, planetary astrophotographer, and texture map creator. I love space perhaps a bit too much, it being an obsession of mine since I was 3 years old.

On YouTube, I make an absurdly wide variety of content, including but not limited to gaming (So far I have played Minecraft, Terraria, Beat Saber, Wobbledogs, My Singing Monsters, and Universe Sanbox 2, more are planned), animation, space size comparisons, nature videos, pets/animals, music, and random shitposts. Often times vidoes are a combination of these things.

Basic Info

I was born on January 20, 2011 in Oregon, and I have never left. I love space and planets and the solar system way too much to be normal, and when I was 13 years old, I got into planetary astrophotography. That combined with the fact that I've been in a lot of artisty fandomy spaces since around 2020, I think it's safe to say I'm a big ol' NERD. I would take being a nerd over being much anything else any day.

I'm generally on the anti-AI side of things and think that human-made work is interently of infinite value. My username "meiscoolbutmo" is historically associated with AI, but this is because I liked the simpler, horrible-at-its job AI that produces hillarious results (basically back when Ai was an experiment) rather than modern AI, which is a huge waste of energy, and furstratingly, coherent enough to pretend like it's being trustwortly or believale at first glance while actually being incredibly factually wrong. Do not confuse past me's opinions on funny experimental AI with current me's opinions on terrible creativity-ruining AI. They are different things.

You may be wondering where the name "meiscoolbutmo" comes from, and people have asked me this before since it's just such an unusual username to commit to. It's true origins trace back to the 28th of May, 2020 when I was making my first account online, specifically my Scratch account. I sat there at the little box asking for your username and thought, "I'm a cool person I like to think", so I typed in "meiscool". Suddenly, 9-year-old me felt a pang of guilt. What if people think I have too big of an ego? What if people critisize me for being conceited? So I quickly added "butno" to negate the claim that I was cool. However, I typed this so quickly that it turned into the typo "meiscoolbutmo", with an m instead of an n. This made people think it was supposed to mean "meiscoolbutmore", as in I was cool and then more cool, yet nobody ever judged me for being cool, so plan failed sucessfully. I have then continued to build my entire online identity around a typo I made in the Year of Infamy and Horrors (2020).

As you may notice, almost every single one of my profile pictures is of King from the Owl House. This is because King is simply amazing. You cannot argue with this fact. Look at him! He looks like a kitty cat and I love cats. I have even made my favicon on this website to be King. Over time, this may make people associate me with King, but do not mistake this for my apperance, as in real life, I am not a sub-meter tall fluffy bone boy. This should be obvious if you really think about it. I don't even really know why I'm specifying something like this, since you can already know this on your own, since it is basic common sense that in real life, I, or anyone else, is not a sub-meter tall fluffy bone boy.

Future Texture Maps

The ideas here are less structured because I kind of just make whatever I want to, slowly building my portfolio. But I do have ideas, and here are those ideas:

• I want to update my Jupiter texture map at some point, or perhaps simply make a new and much better Jupiter map when Hubble observes the planet again as part of the OPAL program.

• I also think it would be cool to make a full texture map of Jupiter not based off of Hubble's OPAL program, but instead based off of data from my own telescope. I have doen a partial map before and it was really cool. I also want to do the same thing with planet Mars.

• I plan to make full texture maps, probably semi-fictional (I would have to take artistic liberties with the poles) of the two ice giants of the solar system, Uranus and Neptune, using either Hubble OPAl data, Keck data, or both.

• In October of 2026, I bought an A4 sheet of neutral solar filter sheet stuff. I plan to use it to make a true-color map of the Sun using data from my own telescope. This would be a month long project, but the result would be really cool.

• In general, I hope to make more fictional texture maps of planets or other objects not well mapped, like some trans-neptunian objects, exoplanets, or hypothetical objects I made up / "alt" planets.
